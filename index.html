Values in society: today and tomorrow

This short survey explores how people think society should balance three values today and in a possible future society. Please use your own judgement; there are no right or wrong answers.

About this survey

One dot in each triangle

Choose one point in each triangle. The closer the point is to a corner, the greater the share given to that value. Your three allocations always add up to 100%.

Corporate controlGovernance, subscription access and AI-managed services.
Environmental protectionSustainability, restoration and ecological limits.
Personal freedomAutonomy, privacy and independence from corporate systems.

How to take part

  1. Read the three values.
  2. Click inside the triangle for society today.
  3. Click inside the triangle for the future society.
  4. Review the allocations and submit once.
