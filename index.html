MIS20070 · Digital Society · Values exercise

Values in society: today and tomorrow

We are a group of students studying Digital Society and exploring how society might change by 2060. We want to understand how people see society’s priorities today and how they think those priorities might look in the future society imagined in our project.

About this exercise

One dot in each of two triangles

You will place one dot in each of two triangles: one for society today and one for the future. Each corner represents a value. Placing your dot closer to a corner gives that value a larger share of society’s priorities. The three shares always add up to 100%. There are no right or wrong answers; we are interested in your own view.

This is a student project about values and imagined futures. The three value labels below are a draft and should be confirmed by our group before the survey is shared.

Corporate controlGovernance, subscription access and AI-managed services.
Environmental protectionSustainability, restoration and ecological limits.
Personal freedomAutonomy, privacy and independence from corporate systems.

How to take part

  1. Enter the one-time access code supplied by the project team.
  2. Choose a balance for society today.
  3. Choose a balance for the future described by the project team.
  4. Review your choices and submit once.

Privacy: this form is designed not to ask for your name or email. Do not enter identifying information in the code field. Your response is stored in the project team's survey database.
